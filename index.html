      About Gin

      Gin is gin rummy for two people, wherever they are. There are no accounts, no ads and no saved games: just a deck of cards and a friend.

      Starting a game

      1. Choose how many points to play to and choose Start a game.
      2. Send the code (or the link) to your friend.
      3. When they join, the cards are dealt: ten each, with one turned up to start the discard pile.

      No one to play? Pick Easy, Medium or Hard and choose Play the computer. It plays right here in your browser.

      Playing a hand

      • Make melds: sets of three or four cards of the same rank, or runs of three or more in a row in one suit. Aces are low, unless whoever started the game chose high (Q-K-A) or both (which also allows runs like K-A-2) in Settings. Each meld in your hand is shown in its own color.
      • Your hand sorts itself into melds (Sort: Auto, the default). Choose Manual to arrange it yourself: drag a card sideways, or tab to it and use the arrow keys. New cards join at the right. Your choice is saved in this browser.
      • Cards that aren’t in a meld are deadwood: aces count 1, picture cards 10, the rest their number.
      • On your turn, take the top card of the stock or the discard pile, then tap a card to pick it up and tap it again (or choose Discard) to throw it away. You can even throw back a card you just took from the pile.
      • To start a hand, the player who didn’t deal may take the upcard or pass; then the dealer may. If both pass, the first player draws from the stock.
      • The goal is gin: after you discard, your ten cards make exactly three melds of four, three and three (a 4-3-3). Other shapes with no deadwood, like two runs of five or a set of three and a run of seven, aren’t gin. There’s no knocking, and nothing to claim: throw your spare card and the hand ends with gin.
      • If the stock gets down to two cards and nobody has gone gin, the hand is a draw.

      Scoring

      • Gin scores 25 plus the other player’s deadwood, in their best arrangement.
      • The first to reach the target wins the game and a 100-point bonus, and each player adds 25 for every hand they won. If the loser won no hands at all, the winner’s total doubles.

      Video and chat

      Turn on your camera or microphone at any time during a game, or just type. Each of you decides separately what to share.

      Privacy

      Gin is a static page. A public PeerJS server introduces your two browsers using the game code; after that, cards, chat, video and audio travel directly between you over WebRTC. Nothing is recorded, and only your color settings are saved, in this browser. The game only ever shows you your own cards, but it’s built for friends: both browsers hold the shuffled deck so they can keep the game in step.

      Leave this game?

      The game ends for both of you and can’t be resumed.
